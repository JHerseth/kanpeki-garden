Garden Stage Mockup

The 22 new flower and vegetable strips and the 5 new trees, placed on the real garden background at the size the game would draw them. Each plant's mature frame is about 1.1 times the height of its current sprite; the mound of soil under every frame takes up part of that height. The apple tree is new, so it is sized like the pear tree. The gnome, lantern, watering can and birdbath are the current decorations, for comparison.

  • Left beds: lettuce and napa cabbage at sprout, young and mature.
  • Top two long beds: flowers in full bloom.
  • Bottom long bed: not reviewed for a while. Hydrangea and lupine are dying, the violet and pansy are dead.
  • Small beds: two sprouts, a young and a mature tulip, a pansy.
  • Tall bed: a mature blueberry bush above a young red berry bush in flower.
  • On the grass: fiddleheads, ivy, moss, toadstools, a lupine, a hydrangea and a dying marguerite.
  • Trees: a mature apple and cherry tree top right, a mature maple bottom left, a young pear tree with blossom, and an orange tree sprout by the path.

The same places, every plant mature.

The same places after days without reviews: most plants dying, every third one dead. A sprout or young plant that dies uses the plant's full-size dying and dead frames, by design.

The beds at full size, one image pixel per screen pixel. This is about what the player sees when zoomed in all the way.

The apple and cherry trees at full size. The trees came from their own sheet and are drawn smoother than the pixel-art background and flowers; this is where the difference shows most.

The garden opens fitted to the screen, so on a phone held upright the whole map is about 412 points wide and a flower is roughly 20 points tall. The player pinches to zoom in.
