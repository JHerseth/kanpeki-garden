Garden Stage Prompts

Image-generator prompts for one life-cycle strip of five frames per plant: 55 plants that had no stage art, 6 redos in the new style (the 5 smooth trees and the bamboo and log), 28 redos of plants from the simpler 40-plant sheet, and 7 new plants for the shop. Each card has the plant's current sprite (or, for new plants, its picture from the 40-plant sheet) to attach as a reference.

  1. Sprout
  2. Young
  3. Mature
  4. Dying
  5. Dead
  1. Attach the 40-plant stage sheet as the style reference.
  2. Attach the plant's reference sprite. Right-click the picture on its card, or take it from art/stage_refs/.
  3. Paste the prompt.
  4. Save the result as the file name on the card, e.g. rose_pink.png.

Left out
