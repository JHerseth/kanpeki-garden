Kanpeki Garden: setup
=====================

A small garden game on the back of your Anki cards. Each card answered in AnkiDroid pays one coin a day;
coins buy plants and decorations for a pixel-art garden.

1. Copy the media files from this zip into the collection.media folder of your Anki profile on the
   computer (the folder next to the profile's collection.anki2):
     _garden.js
     _garden_sprites1.webp (and any further _garden_sprites<n>.webp)
     _garden_bg.webp
   Their names start with "_", so Tools > Check Media counts them as used and never deletes them.

2. Paste the contents of template-snippet.html at the end of the back template of each card type that
   should pay coins (Browse > Cards... > Back Template).

3. On the phone, in AnkiDroid (2.23 or later): Settings > "New study screen" > turn on the "New study screen"
   switch at the top of that page. It's off by default, and the garden shows only in the new study screen.

4. Still in AnkiDroid: Settings > Advanced > turn on "localStorage in Study Screen" (under Workarounds).
   The garden keeps its save there; without it nothing is saved.

5. Sync, then study on the phone: a coin chip shows in the bottom-right corner on the back of each card.
   Tap it to open the garden.

Good to know
- The garden shows only in AnkiDroid's new study screen (AnkiDroid 2.23 or later), not in desktop Anki,
  the previewer or the card browser.
- The garden and its coins are saved on that phone only; they are not synced. Clearing AnkiDroid's app
  data, or uninstalling it, deletes them.
- Away for a while? Turn on おやすみ (vacation) in the garden's settings (the gear): plants then don't
  grow, wilt or die, and cards pay no coins until it's turned off.
- To update, replace the media files with the new zip's; the garden's save is kept.
- The version is shown at the bottom of the garden's settings.
